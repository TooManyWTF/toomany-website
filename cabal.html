How to read this page

Cabal Score. A 0–100 risk score for how “caballed” the visible supply looks. Lower is better. The Cabal Score looks at how much supply sits in whales, exchanges, bridges, LPs and project wallets versus many smaller community holders. It only uses the top holders we can see on-chain.

How to Read This Fingerprint

  • Whale Gravity – how much control the largest whales have; farther out = fewer wallets hold more power.
  • CEX Custody Heat – how much supply sits on exchanges; higher = more centralized exchange risk.
  • Bridge Dependency – how bridge-heavy the token is; higher = more supply locked in bridges.
  • LP Stability – how fragile the LP looks; higher = easier to nuke or manipulate liquidity.
  • Holder Churn – how fast wallets rotate; higher = degen / bot-heavy trading, less diamond hands.
  • Cluster Density – how much power sits in shared infra (CEX, LPs, bridges, project multisigs).
  • Top 10 Compression – how concentrated the top 10 wallets are; higher = more cabal tension at the top.
  • Fresh Wallet Velocity – how quickly new wallets rush in; higher = hype / momentum inflow speed.

Bigger spikes mean stronger influence. Smooth, rounded shapes are healthier; jagged, lopsided shapes point to cabal-heavy risk.

Snapshot of Supply Breaks the visible supply into buckets like Community, Whales, CEX custody, Bridges, DEX / LP, Project / Treasury and Burned. Percentages are based on the top holders returned by the API, after tagging known CEX, LP, bridge, project and burn wallets.

Custody Clusters Groups big shared wallets (centralized exchanges, LP pools, project multisigs, bridges) into clusters so you can see how much supply those infrastructures jointly control. A large cluster means a single failure or freeze could affect a big chunk of supply.

Top Holders Summary Shows how concentrated the largest wallets are: top 10 / 20 / 50 holders and whale wallets. Whale wallets are non-infrastructure, non-exchange holders above our whale threshold of the supply. Higher percentages here mean more power in fewer hands.

Top Holders Table A wallet-by-wallet view of the largest holders we can see. Tags like Whale, CEX, DEX / LP, Project, Bridge, Burn and Contract explain what each address looks like. “View” opens the wallet on a block explorer for deeper manual research.
